Model Evaluation & Limitations

This model was trained on historical machine examples from a synthetic benchmark dataset. While it accurately reproduces documented physical failure modes (thermal stress, electrical overload, mechanical strain, and tool degradation), synthetic benchmarks cannot capture all unmodeled real-world factory variables such as structural vibration, lubricant degradation, or sensor calibration drift. It should not be used as the sole basis for real plant safety decisions.
